## Discussion Conclusion
The focus group discussion revealed that manufacturing SMEs in Nigeria face significant challenges in innovation adoption, including high costs, skills gaps, regulatory complexities, and infrastructure challenges. However, they also recognize the importance of innovation for competitiveness and are willing to invest in it despite the challenges. The participants emphasized the need for government support, improved infrastructure, and better collaboration between manufacturers and industry associations.
