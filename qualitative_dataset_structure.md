# Qualitative Dataset Structure for Nigerian SME Innovation Adoption Research

## Dataset Overview
This dataset contains primary qualitative data collected through semi-structured interviews and focus group discussions with Nigerian SME owners and managers. The data explores innovation adoption patterns, constraints, and their impact on business performance.

## Data Collection Methods

### 1. Semi-Structured Interviews
- **Sample Size**: 25 participants
- **Duration**: 45-90 minutes per interview
- **Format**: One-on-one interviews
- **Focus**: Deep dive into individual innovation journeys

### 2. Focus Group Discussions (FGDs)
- **Number of Groups**: 5 FGDs
- **Group Size**: 6-8 participants per group
- **Duration**: 90-120 minutes per group
- **Sector Groupings**: 
  - Technology/ICT SMEs
  - Agribusiness SMEs
  - Manufacturing SMEs
  - Retail/Commerce SMEs
  - Financial Services SMEs

## Key Research Themes
1. Innovation adoption decision-making processes
2. Barriers and constraints to innovation
3. Role of government policies and support
4. Impact on business performance (sales, costs, customer relationships)
5. Strategies for overcoming innovation challenges
6. Sector-specific innovation patterns

## Data Files Structure
- `interviews/` - Individual interview transcripts
- `focus_groups/` - FGD transcripts by sector
- `metadata/` - Participant demographics and business characteristics
- `analysis_framework/` - Coding schemes and analysis guidelines
