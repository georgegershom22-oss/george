## 📊 Core Data Files (CSV Format)

### 1. `sofc_performance_characteristics.csv` 
**Records**: 26 SOFC systems  
**Key Metrics**: Electrical efficiency (35-65% LHV), thermal efficiency (30-40%), degradation rates (0.35-1.5%/1000h), stack lifespan (45,000-78,000h)  
**Applications**: Performance modeling, efficiency analysis, technology comparison

### 2. `sofc_fuel_flexibility_nigeria.csv`
**Records**: 20 Nigerian fuel types  
**Key Metrics**: Fuel compositions, efficiency impacts, processing requirements, regional availability  
**Coverage**: Pipeline NG, associated gas, biogas sources, LPG, flare gas, alternative fuels  
**Applications**: Fuel resource assessment, preprocessing design, efficiency optimization

### 3. `sofc_power_density_sizing.csv`
**Records**: 30 SOFC configurations  
**Key Metrics**: Power density (1.8-6.0 kW/m²), volumetric density (120-500 kW/m³), footprint requirements  
**Technologies**: Planar, tubular, micro-tubular, metal-supported systems  
**Applications**: System sizing, footprint planning, technology selection

### 4. `sofc_operational_characteristics.csv`
**Records**: 30 system types  
**Key Metrics**: Start-up times (8-720 min), ramp rates (1.5-6.0%/min), cycling capability, backup power suitability  
**Applications**: Grid integration, backup power design, load following analysis

### 5. `nigeria_specific_sofc_requirements.csv`
**Records**: 30 Nigerian locations  
**Key Metrics**: Climate conditions, grid stability, infrastructure quality, SOFC suitability scores  
**Coverage**: All climate zones from coastal to Sahel regions  
**Applications**: Site selection, system adaptation, regional deployment planning

### 6. `sofc_manufacturer_specifications.csv`
**Records**: 30 manufacturers/initiatives  
**Key Metrics**: Technology types, power ranges, costs ($2,000-32,000/kW), market presence, Nigerian partnerships  
**Coverage**: International manufacturers, Nigerian initiatives, academic research  
**Applications**: Vendor selection, partnership development, market analysis

### 7. `sofc_economic_data.csv`
**Records**: 20 economic scenarios  
**Key Metrics**: Capital costs, LCOE ($112-395/MWh), payback periods (5.8-22.5 years), economic viability  
**Applications**: Business case development, policy analysis, investment decisions

### 8. `sofc_environmental_impact.csv`
**Records**: 20 environmental scenarios  
**Key Metrics**: CO2 emissions (0-920 kg/MWh), carbon credit potential, environmental compliance  
**Applications**: Environmental impact assessment, carbon credit analysis, regulatory compliance

## 🎯 Key Dataset Highlights

### Technical Performance
- **Efficiency Range**: 35-65% electrical (LHV), up to 91.4% combined
- **Power Density**: Up to 6.0 kW/m² for micro-tubular systems
- **Degradation**: As low as 0.35%/1000h for advanced systems
- **Lifespan**: Up to 78,000 hours operational life

### Nigerian Fuel Resources
- **20 fuel types analyzed** including pipeline NG, biogas, LPG, flare gas
- **Efficiency impacts** range from +8.5% (hydrogen) to -25.2% (producer gas)
- **High availability** fuels: Associated gas, flare gas, agricultural biogas
- **Processing requirements** detailed for each fuel type

### Economic Viability
- **Most viable**: Flare gas recovery (LCOE $112/MWh, 5.8-year payback)
- **Excellent prospects**: Industrial biogas, utility NG systems
- **Cost range**: $2,000-32,000/kW depending on application and scale
- **Financing**: Subsidies of $400-2,000/kW could significantly improve viability

### Environmental Benefits
- **CO2 reduction**: 60-70% vs diesel generators
- **Carbon negative potential**: Biogas and flare gas applications
- **Low emissions**: NOx 1-45 g/MWh, SOx 0-25 g/MWh
- **Waste heat recovery**: 75-95% efficiency possible

### Regional Suitability
- **30 Nigerian locations** analyzed across all climate zones
- **Suitability scores**: 3.2-7.8 (Jos highest, Gashua lowest)
- **Technology recommendations** by climate zone
- **Special requirements** for each environmental condition
