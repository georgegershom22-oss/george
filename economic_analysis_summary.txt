================================================================================
RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

Based on the techno-economic analysis:

1. SOFC Technology shows promise for:
   - Baseload power applications (>80% capacity factor)
   - Areas with reliable natural gas supply
   - High efficiency requirements (combined heat & power)
   - Long-term cost stability (lower fuel price volatility)

2. Diesel Generators remain competitive for:
   - Backup/emergency power (<40% capacity factor)
   - Lower capital availability scenarios
   - Short-term/temporary installations
   - Remote areas without gas infrastructure

3. Solar + Battery systems are optimal for:
   - Daytime load profiles
   - Locations with high solar irradiance
   - Environmental compliance requirements
   - Decreasing costs trajectory

4. Policy Recommendations:
   - Implement gas supply guarantees for SOFC projects
   - Provide CAPEX subsidies or low-interest financing
   - Establish feed-in tariffs for clean generation
   - Streamline import duties on fuel cell equipment
   - Develop local content and manufacturing capacity
