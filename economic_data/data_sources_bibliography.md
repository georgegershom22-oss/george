## Data Validation and Cross-References

### Quality Assurance Methods:

1. **Multiple Source Verification:**
   - Each cost data point verified against 2-3 sources
   - Outliers investigated and documented

2. **Consistency Checks:**
   - Cross-technology comparisons
   - Historical trend analysis
   - Regional price adjustments

3. **Expert Review:**
   - Industry expert consultations
   - Academic peer review
   - Manufacturer validation where possible

4. **Currency and Time Adjustments:**
   - All costs converted to USD (October 2024)
   - Exchange rate: 1,625 NGN/USD (CBN official)
   - Inflation adjustments applied where necessary

5. **Nigerian Context Adjustments:**
   - Import duties added (5-10%)
   - Shipping and logistics costs included
   - Installation premium for Nigeria (+20-30%)
   - Currency risk premium included

## Limitations and Disclaimers

1. **Data Currency:**
   - Prices and rates as of October 2024
   - Rapid changes in Nigeria economy may affect accuracy
   - Exchange rate volatility significant

2. **Market Conditions:**
   - Limited SOFC deployment history in Africa
   - Projections based on international experience
   - Local conditions may vary significantly

3. **Policy Environment:**
   - Nigerian policy environment evolving
   - Incentives subject to change
   - Regulatory framework developing

4. **Technology Evolution:**
   - SOFC costs declining rapidly
   - Battery costs falling faster than projected
   - Solar PV costs continuing to decrease
