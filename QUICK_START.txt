💡 KEY INSIGHTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. IMC THICKNESS: Optimal ~2 µm (too thin = poor bonding, too thick = brittle)

2. ENERGY DENSITY: Moderate levels avoid defects while ensuring bonding

3. FORCE: Most important predictor (21-23% importance across all targets)

4. THERMAL CYCLING: Degradation is non-linear with initial quality

5. TECHNIQUE TRADE-OFFS:
   → CMW: Highest pass rate (23.1%) but slower
   → USW: Good balance of quality and speed (16.6%)
   → Laser/RSW: Fast but challenging quality (3.8%)

  ├─ Material:            6 (anode, cathode, thickness, finish)
  ├─ Process:             9 (power, force, time, etc.)
  └─ Environmental:       3 (temperature, humidity)

Characterization:        15 features
  ├─ Geometry:            4 (width, length, penetration, grain size)
  ├─ Mechanical:          2 (shear strength, peel strength)
  ├─ Electrical:          1 (contact resistance)
  ├─ Defects:             3 (porosity, cracks, voids)
  ├─ Microstructure:      1 (IMC thickness)
  ├─ Thermal:             2 (peak temp, cooling rate)
  └─ Surface:             1 (roughness)

Performance:             8 features
  ├─ Degradation:         6 (resistance, strength, fatigue, etc.)
  └─ Quality:             2 (score, pass/fail)

Metadata:                5 features
  └─ sample_id, timestamp, batch_id, energy_density, quality_class
