# SOFC Technical Dataset for Nigeria - Complete Summary

## 🎯 Dataset Successfully Generated

I have successfully created a comprehensive technical and technological dataset for Solid Oxide Fuel Cell (SOFC) systems tailored for Nigeria's electricity crisis analysis. The dataset is now ready for use in your techno-economic and socio-political analysis.

## 📊 Dataset Contents

### Core Data Files Created:

1. **Performance Characteristics** (`sofc_performance_characteristics.json` - 5.6 KB)
   - Electrical efficiency curves (45-60% LHV)
   - Thermal efficiency for CHP (up to 85% total)
   - Degradation profiles over 80,000 hour lifetime
   - Temperature dependency analysis

2. **Fuel Flexibility Specifications** (`fuel_flexibility_specifications.json` - 8.4 KB)
   - 5 fuel types analyzed (Natural Gas, Associated Gas, LPG, Biogas, Biomethane)
   - Composition and performance data
   - Nigeria-specific availability mapping
   - Processing requirements

3. **Power Density & Operational Data** (`power_density_operational_characteristics.json` - 10.1 KB)
   - Cell, stack, and system-level power densities
   - Startup characteristics (2-8 hours cold start)
   - Ramp rates and load-following capabilities
   - Nigeria climate impact factors

4. **Manufacturer Specifications** (`manufacturer_technical_specifications.json` - 15.9 KB)
   - 7 major manufacturers analyzed
   - Power range: 1.5 kW to 10 MW
   - Cost range: $3,500-6,000/kW
   - Nigeria suitability ratings (6.5-8.5/10)

5. **Nigeria Deployment Scenarios** (`nigeria_operational_scenarios.json` - 14.7 KB)
   - 8 specific application scenarios
   - Regional demand profiles (Lagos, Kano, Port Harcourt, Abuja)
   - Economic analysis with payback periods
   - Grid integration strategies
