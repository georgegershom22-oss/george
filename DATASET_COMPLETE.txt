🎯 KEY FINDINGS:
================================================================================

1. ⭐ SOFC HAS LOWEST LCOE ($0.116/kWh)
   - 70% cheaper than diesel ($0.387/kWh)
   - 35% cheaper than solar+battery ($0.177/kWh)
   - Most economical for baseload power

2. ⭐ SOFC IS ONLY POSITIVE NPV TECHNOLOGY
   - +$62,192 NPV over 20 years
   - Diesel: -$6.6M (massive losses due to fuel costs)
   - Solar: -$366k (limited by low capacity factor)

3. ⭐ SOFC HAS BEST IRR AND PAYBACK
   - IRR: 10.57% (above cost of capital)
   - Payback: 8.2 years (less than half project life)
   - Diesel: negative cash flows, no payback
   - Solar: 5.34% IRR, 13.6 year payback

4. 📈 SOFC ADVANTAGES INCREASE WITH SCALE
   - 1000 kW SOFC: $0.097/kWh LCOE, +$1.1M NPV, 15.9% IRR
   - Economies of scale favor SOFC
   - Best for 500-1000 kW applications

5. ⚡ CAPACITY FACTOR IS CRITICAL
   - SOFC excels at >60% capacity factor (baseload)
   - Solar limited to 22% CF (daylight only)
   - Diesel viable only for backup (<40% CF)

6. 💰 FUEL COSTS DOMINATE DIESEL ECONOMICS
   - Diesel fuel: $1.1M/year (85% of total costs)
   - SOFC fuel: $72k/year (40% of total costs)
   - Solar: $0 fuel cost (but low output)

7. 🌍 ENVIRONMENTAL BENEFITS
   - SOFC: 51% lower emissions than diesel
   - SOFC: 39% lower than Nigerian grid average
   - Solar: 88% lower than SOFC (but capacity limited)

8. 💼 HIGH CAPEX BUT STRONG RETURNS
   - SOFC CAPEX: 6x higher than diesel
   - But recovers investment through fuel savings
   - Positive NPV demonstrates long-term viability

USE CASE RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

✅ SOFC IS BEST FOR:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Baseload power (>80% capacity factor)
• 24/7 continuous operations (manufacturing, data centers)
• Industrial facilities with high reliability needs
• Locations with reliable natural gas supply
• Combined heat & power applications
• Long-term installations (>10 years)
• Emissions-sensitive applications
• Total cost of ownership focus

✅ DIESEL IS BEST FOR:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Backup/emergency power (<40% capacity factor)
• Peak shaving applications
• Temporary installations
• Remote areas without gas infrastructure
• Lower upfront capital constraints
• Short-term projects (<5 years)
• Flexibility and mobility requirements

✅ SOLAR+BATTERY IS BEST FOR:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Daytime load profiles (offices, retail)
• Strong environmental mandates
• High solar irradiance locations
• Declining cost trajectory advantage
• Zero fuel cost preference
• Grid independence goals
• Combination with other technologies
