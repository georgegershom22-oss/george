## Data Synthesis Methodology

This dataset synthesizes information from the above sources using the following methodology:

1. **Manufacturer baseline data** from publicly available specifications (Sources 1-14)

2. **Performance modeling** based on electrochemical principles and validated models (Sources 15-19)

3. **Degradation patterns** from long-term field studies and accelerated testing (Sources 20-24)

4. **Fuel flexibility** from experimental studies and thermodynamic calculations (Sources 25-29)

5. **Operational characteristics** from manufacturer specifications and dynamic modeling studies (Sources 30-33)

6. **Nigeria-specific adaptations** from climate data (51-52), fuel quality data (48-50), and energy sector reports (45-47, 54-58)

7. **Validation** against IEA, DOE, and academic benchmarks (Sources 34-40)

## Notes on Data Quality & Limitations

### High-Quality Source Data (Direct Manufacturer/Field Data):
- Bloom Energy ES5700 efficiency and performance
- Siemens tubular SOFC field test results
- IEA global deployment statistics
- DOE SECA program performance targets

### Modeled/Estimated Data:
- Degradation rates for newer systems (limited long-term data)
- Nigeria-specific performance derating (limited local deployment)
- Fuel flexibility for some non-standard fuels
- Start-up times for unreleased products

### Data Gaps Requiring Future Validation:
- Long-term (>60,000 hour) degradation in tropical climates
- APG fuel composition variability across Nigerian oil fields
- Actual grid stability metrics (site-specific)
- Local maintenance requirements and costs
