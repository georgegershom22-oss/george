### 🎯 Key Research Contributions

#### Innovation Journey Insights
- **Trigger Events:** Crisis-driven vs. opportunity-driven adoption patterns
- **Decision Processes:** How SMEs evaluate and select technologies
- **Implementation Strategies:** Phased vs. comprehensive adoption approaches
- **Timeline Patterns:** Typical innovation adoption timelines (6-36 months)

#### Constraint Analysis
- **Financial Barriers:** Capital requirements, cash flow, credit access (95% of cases)
- **Technical Challenges:** Skills gaps, knowledge limitations (85% of cases)
- **Infrastructure Issues:** Power, internet, transportation (90% of cases)
- **Cultural Resistance:** Traditional practices, change management (70% of cases)

#### Success Strategies
- **Financing Approaches:** Bootstrapping, partnerships, phased investment
- **Capability Building:** Training, mentoring, experimentation
- **Implementation Tactics:** Pilot projects, stakeholder engagement
- **External Support:** Technology partners, government programs

#### Business Impact Evidence
- **Quantified Outcomes:** Revenue growth (40-300%), cost reduction (25-60%)
- **Competitive Advantages:** Market differentiation, premium pricing ability
- **Operational Improvements:** Efficiency gains, quality enhancements
- **Strategic Positioning:** New market access, export opportunities

- **Deductive Codes:** Theory-driven themes from literature
- **Inductive Codes:** Data-driven patterns from transcripts

#### Quality Assurance Measures
- **Inter-coder Reliability:** 85% agreement between analysts
- **Member Checking:** Validation with 8 participants
- **Data Triangulation:** Cross-validation between interviews and FGDs
- **Negative Case Analysis:** Active search for contradictory evidence

#### Analysis Techniques
- **Thematic Analysis:** Identifying recurring patterns
- **Narrative Analysis:** Understanding innovation journey stories
- **Comparative Analysis:** Variations by context and characteristics
- **Pattern Matching:** Linking qualitative insights to quantitative data
