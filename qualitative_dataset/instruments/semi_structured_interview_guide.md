# Semi-Structured Interview Guide
## Innovation Adoption and Constraints in Nigerian SMEs

### Research Topic
Leveraging Machine Learning to Examine Innovation Adoption and Constraints in Nigerian SMEs: Implications for Performance and Growth

### Interview Duration
60-90 minutes

### Pre-Interview Setup
- Obtain informed consent and permission to record
- Explain confidentiality and data usage
- Collect basic demographic and business information
- Set up recording equipment and backup

---

## SECTION A: PARTICIPANT AND BUSINESS BACKGROUND (10 minutes)

### Basic Information
1. Tell me about yourself and your educational background
2. Describe your business - what do you do, when did you start, how many employees?
3. What sector/industry would you classify your business in?
4. What is your role in the business? (owner, manager, etc.)

### Business Context
5. What is your approximate annual revenue range?
6. How would you describe your business size? (micro, small, medium)
7. What geographic markets do you serve?
8. Who are your main competitors?

---

## SECTION B: INNOVATION JOURNEY - THE STORY (20-25 minutes)

### The Decision Point
9. **Tell me the story of when you decided to adopt [specific technology/innovation].** 
   - What was happening in your business at that time?
   - What triggered this decision?
   - Walk me through your thought process

### The Innovation Details
10. **Describe the specific technology or innovation you adopted.**
    - What exactly did you implement?
    - Why this particular solution?
    - What alternatives did you consider?

11. **What were the biggest hurdles you faced in making this transition?**
    - Technical challenges
    - Financial barriers
    - Skills/knowledge gaps
    - Market resistance
    - Regulatory issues

### Navigation Strategies
12. **How did you navigate these challenges?**
    - What strategies did you use?
    - Who helped you?
    - What resources did you tap into?
    - How long did the process take?

---

## SECTION C: BUSINESS IMPACT AND OUTCOMES (15-20 minutes)

### Performance Impact
13. **How did this innovation specifically affect your sales?**
    - Quantify the changes if possible
    - Timeline of impact
    - Which customer segments were affected?

14. **What was the impact on your costs and operational efficiency?**
    - Direct cost changes
    - Operational improvements
    - Resource utilization changes

15. **How did it affect your customer relationships?**
    - Customer satisfaction changes
    - New customer acquisition
    - Customer retention improvements
    - Service quality changes

### Competitive Position
16. **How has this innovation affected your competitive position?**
    - Market share changes
    - Competitive advantages gained
    - New market opportunities
    - Differentiation from competitors

---

## SECTION D: EXTERNAL FACTORS AND CONSTRAINTS (10-15 minutes)

### Government and Policy
17. **What role did government policies or support play in your innovation journey?**
    - Supportive policies or programs
    - Regulatory barriers or challenges
    - Access to government funding or incentives
    - Policy uncertainty impacts

### Infrastructure and Environment
18. **How did infrastructure challenges affect your innovation adoption?**
    - Power supply issues
    - Internet connectivity
    - Transportation and logistics
    - Skills availability in the market

### Financing and Investment
19. **Tell me about the financing aspects of your innovation.**
    - How did you fund the innovation?
    - What financing challenges did you face?
    - What would have made financing easier?

---

## SECTION E: UNEXPECTED OUTCOMES AND LEARNING (10 minutes)

### Surprises and Unintended Consequences
20. **What unexpected challenges emerged that you hadn't anticipated?**

21. **What unexpected benefits or opportunities arose from this innovation?**

22. **If you were to do this again, what would you do differently?**

### Knowledge and Capabilities
23. **How has this innovation changed your business capabilities?**
    - New skills developed
    - New processes established
    - Changed business model elements

---

## SECTION F: FUTURE OUTLOOK AND ADVICE (10 minutes)

### Future Innovation Plans
24. **What's next for your innovation journey?**
    - Technologies you're considering
    - Expansion plans
    - New capabilities you want to develop

### Advice and Recommendations
25. **What advice would you give to other SME owners considering similar innovations?**

26. **What would most help SMEs like yours to innovate successfully?**
    - Policy recommendations
    - Support mechanisms needed
    - Infrastructure improvements

---

## SECTION G: FOLLOW-UP AND CLARIFICATION (5 minutes)

### Additional Context
27. **Is there anything important about your innovation journey that we haven't discussed?**

28. **Are there any specific examples or stories that illustrate your points?**

### Network and Referrals
29. **Can you recommend other SME owners who have interesting innovation stories?**

30. **Would you be willing to participate in follow-up research if needed?**

---

## POST-INTERVIEW NOTES

### Interviewer Observations
- Participant engagement level
- Key themes that emerged
- Notable quotes or insights
- Non-verbal cues and context
- Technical understanding level
- Emotional responses to different topics

### Follow-up Actions
- Additional documents requested
- Referrals to contact
- Clarifications needed
- Potential case study opportunities

---

## PROBING QUESTIONS AND TECHNIQUES

### For Deeper Exploration
- "Can you give me a specific example of that?"
- "What do you mean by...?"
- "How did that make you feel?"
- "What was going through your mind at that time?"
- "Can you walk me through that process step by step?"

### For Quantification
- "Can you put a number on that?"
- "How much did that cost/save?"
- "What percentage change did you see?"
- "How long did that take?"

### For Context and Comparison
- "How does that compare to your competitors?"
- "How is that different from before?"
- "What would have happened if you hadn't made this change?"
- "How does this compare to other investments you've made?"

### For Future Orientation
- "Where do you see this leading?"
- "What are the implications of this?"
- "How might this change in the future?"
- "What would need to happen for you to...?"

---

## ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS

### Informed Consent
- Explain research purpose and data usage
- Obtain permission for recording
- Clarify confidentiality protections
- Allow withdrawal at any time

### Data Protection
- Anonymize sensitive business information
- Secure storage of recordings and transcripts
- Limited access to identifiable data
- Clear data retention and destruction policies

### Reciprocity
- Offer to share relevant research findings
- Provide business insights where appropriate
- Respect participant time and expertise
- Acknowledge contributions appropriately
