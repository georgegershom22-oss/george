# Focus Group Discussion Protocol
## Innovation Adoption and Constraints in Nigerian SMEs

### Research Topic
Leveraging Machine Learning to Examine Innovation Adoption and Constraints in Nigerian SMEs: Implications for Performance and Growth

### FGD Duration
90-120 minutes

### Participants
6-8 SME owners/managers from similar sectors or regions

### Pre-FGD Setup
- Arrange seating in circle or U-shape for interaction
- Test recording equipment and backup systems
- Prepare name tags and participant information sheets
- Set up refreshments and comfortable environment
- Brief assistant moderator on roles and responsibilities

---

## FACILITATOR GUIDELINES

### Key Principles
- Encourage all participants to contribute
- Manage dominant personalities tactfully
- Probe for specific examples and details
- Allow natural conversation flow while maintaining focus
- Capture group dynamics and consensus building
- Note disagreements and different perspectives

### Facilitation Techniques
- Use open-ended questions
- Employ the "round-robin" technique for equal participation
- Use "building" techniques ("What does everyone else think about that?")
- Summarize and reflect back key points
- Use silence effectively to encourage deeper responses

---

## INTRODUCTION AND WARM-UP (15 minutes)

### Welcome and Introductions
1. **Welcome participants and explain the purpose**
   - Research objectives and importance
   - How findings will be used
   - Confidentiality assurances

2. **Ground Rules**
   - One person speaks at a time
   - All opinions are valuable
   - Respect different viewpoints
   - Mobile phones on silent
   - Permission to record

3. **Round of Introductions**
   - Name and business type
   - Years in business
   - One word describing your innovation experience

---

## SECTION A: INNOVATION EXPERIENCES AND STORIES (25 minutes)

### Opening Question
4. **Let's start by going around the room. Tell us about one major innovation or technology you've adopted in your business in the past 3 years.**
   - What was it?
   - Why did you choose it?
   - What was the outcome?

*[Allow each participant 2-3 minutes, then open for general discussion]*

### Follow-up Discussion
5. **What similarities do you see in each other's experiences?**

6. **What differences stand out to you?**

7. **Who has had the most challenging innovation journey? Tell us more about that.**

---

## SECTION B: COMMON CONSTRAINTS AND BARRIERS (20 minutes)

### Barrier Identification
8. **What are the biggest barriers you face as a group when trying to adopt new technologies or innovations?**
   - *Probe for: financing, skills, infrastructure, regulation, market acceptance*

9. **Let's prioritize these barriers. If you had to rank the top 3 barriers affecting SMEs in your sector, what would they be?**
   - *Use flip chart to capture and rank*

### Sector-Specific Challenges
10. **Are there barriers that are unique to your sector/region that others might not understand?**

11. **How do these barriers compare to what larger companies face?**

### Consensus Building
12. **Where do we have strong agreement on the biggest challenges?**

13. **Where do we have different perspectives? Let's explore those differences.**

---

## SECTION C: GOVERNMENT POLICY AND SUPPORT (20 minutes)

### Policy Impact Assessment
14. **How have government policies affected your innovation journeys - both positively and negatively?**
   - *Encourage specific examples and stories*

15. **What government support have you actually accessed versus what you've heard about?**

16. **Where do you see gaps between policy intentions and real-world implementation?**

### Collective Experiences
17. **Have any of you worked together or with government agencies on innovation initiatives?**

18. **What would be the most helpful government intervention for SMEs like yours?**

### Regional/Sectoral Perspectives
19. **Do government policies affect different regions or sectors differently? How?**

20. **What policies exist on paper but don't work in practice?**

---

## SECTION D: STRATEGIES AND SOLUTIONS (20 minutes)

### Successful Strategies
21. **What strategies have worked for you in overcoming innovation barriers?**
   - *Look for common approaches and unique solutions*

22. **Who has found creative ways to finance innovation? Share those strategies.**

23. **How do you handle the skills and knowledge gaps when adopting new technologies?**

### Collaborative Approaches
24. **Have any of you collaborated with other SMEs on innovation projects?**

25. **What role do industry associations or business networks play in your innovation efforts?**

26. **How do you share knowledge and learn from each other?**

### Resource Sharing
27. **What resources do you wish you could share with other SMEs?**

28. **Where do you see opportunities for collective action on innovation challenges?**

---

## SECTION E: BUSINESS IMPACT AND PERFORMANCE (15 minutes)

### Performance Outcomes
29. **Let's talk about results. How has innovation adoption specifically affected your business performance?**
   - *Probe for: sales, costs, efficiency, competitiveness*

30. **Who has seen the most dramatic business transformation from innovation? Tell us about that.**

31. **What unexpected benefits have emerged from your innovation efforts?**

### Competitive Dynamics
32. **How has innovation changed the competitive landscape in your sector?**

33. **Are you competing differently now than before you adopted these innovations?**

34. **What advantages do innovative SMEs have over traditional ones?**

---

## SECTION F: FUTURE OUTLOOK AND EMERGING TECHNOLOGIES (10 minutes)

### Future Technologies
35. **What emerging technologies are you most interested in or concerned about?**

36. **What innovations do you think will be game-changers for SMEs in Nigeria?**

37. **What's holding you back from adopting the next wave of innovations?**

### Collective Vision
38. **If you could design the ideal innovation ecosystem for Nigerian SMEs, what would it look like?**

39. **What would need to change for innovation adoption to become easier and more successful?**

---

## SECTION G: SYNTHESIS AND CLOSING (10 minutes)

### Key Themes Summary
40. **Let me summarize what I've heard as the key themes. Do these resonate with everyone?**
   - *Present 3-4 main themes and get group validation*

41. **What's the most important insight that's emerged from our discussion today?**

42. **Is there anything critical we haven't discussed about innovation in Nigerian SMEs?**

### Action Orientation
43. **If you could send one message to policymakers about SME innovation, what would it be?**

44. **What's one thing you'll do differently in your business based on today's discussion?**

### Closing
45. **Any final thoughts or advice for other SME owners who might read about this research?**

---

## POST-FGD ACTIVITIES

### Immediate Debrief (with assistant moderator)
- Key themes and insights
- Group dynamics observations
- Dominant and quiet participants
- Consensus and disagreement areas
- Surprising or unexpected findings
- Technical or recording issues

### Follow-up Actions
- Transcription arrangements
- Participant thank you notes
- Additional contacts or referrals
- Clarification needs
- Document any promises made to participants

## SPECIFIC PROBING TECHNIQUES

### For Deeper Exploration
- "Can someone give us a specific example of that?"
- "What does everyone else think about [participant's] experience?"
- "How many others have had similar experiences?"
- "Let's hear from someone who might have a different perspective"

### For Consensus Building
- "Where do we all agree on this issue?"
- "What are the different viewpoints in the room?"
- "Can we find common ground on this?"
- "How do we reconcile these different experiences?"

### For Quantification and Specificity
- "Can anyone put numbers on that impact?"
- "What would that look like in practical terms?"
- "How long did that process take for you?"
- "What did that cost compared to the benefits?"

### For Future Orientation
- "Where do you see this heading?"
- "What would need to happen for that to change?"
- "How might this look different in 5 years?"
- "What would you do if you were starting over today?"

---

## MANAGING GROUP DYNAMICS

### Encouraging Participation
- Direct questions to quiet participants
- Use non-verbal encouragement (eye contact, nodding)
- "We haven't heard from [name] on this topic"
- "Does anyone have a different experience?"

### Managing Dominant Participants
- "Let's hear from others on this"
- "Thank you [name], what do others think?"
- Use body language to redirect attention
- Set time limits for individual responses

### Handling Disagreements
- "It sounds like we have different experiences here"
- "Both perspectives seem valid - can you help us understand the differences?"
- "What might explain these different outcomes?"
- Avoid taking sides or judging responses

### Maintaining Focus
- "That's interesting - how does that relate to innovation adoption?"
- "Let's come back to the question of..."
- "Before we move on, let's make sure we've fully explored..."
- Use agenda visibility to redirect when needed

---

## ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS

### Informed Consent
- Group consent process at beginning
- Individual right to withdraw
- Confidentiality within group limitations
- Recording permissions from all participants

### Confidentiality Management
- Remind participants about confidentiality
- Handle sensitive business information carefully
- Anonymize in transcripts and reports
- Secure data storage and access

### Power Dynamics
- Be aware of hierarchies within group
- Ensure equal speaking opportunities
- Protect vulnerable participants
- Manage competitive tensions between participants

### Cultural Sensitivity
- Respect local customs and languages
- Allow for cultural expressions and examples
- Be sensitive to religious or traditional perspectives
- Adapt facilitation style to cultural context
