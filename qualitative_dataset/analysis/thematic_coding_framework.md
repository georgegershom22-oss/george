# Thematic Coding Framework
## Innovation Adoption and Constraints in Nigerian SMEs

### Research Question
How do Nigerian SMEs navigate innovation adoption processes, and what factors influence their success in leveraging technology for performance and growth?

---

## CODING STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

### Level 1: Major Themes (Deductive)
Pre-defined themes based on research objectives and literature review

### Level 2: Sub-themes (Mixed)
Combination of deductive (theory-driven) and inductive (data-driven) codes

### Level 3: Descriptive Codes (Inductive)
Detailed codes emerging directly from data analysis

---

## MAJOR THEME 1: INNOVATION ADOPTION JOURNEY

### Definition
The process, decisions, and experiences involved in adopting new technologies or innovations

### Sub-themes

#### 1.1 Trigger Events and Decision Points
**Definition:** Specific incidents, problems, or opportunities that prompted innovation adoption

**Codes:**
- `TRIGGER_crisis` - Business crisis or failure that necessitated change
- `TRIGGER_opportunity` - Market opportunity or competitive advantage sought
- `TRIGGER_customer` - Customer demands or complaints driving change
- `TRIGGER_efficiency` - Internal inefficiency or cost pressures
- `TRIGGER_regulation` - Regulatory requirements or compliance needs
- `TRIGGER_growth` - Business expansion or scaling requirements
- `TRIGGER_technology` - Exposure to new technology or demonstration
- `TRIGGER_peer` - Influence from other businesses or industry examples

#### 1.2 Innovation Selection Process
**Definition:** How SMEs choose specific technologies or innovations to adopt

**Codes:**
- `SELECT_research` - Systematic research and evaluation process
- `SELECT_recommendation` - Recommendations from advisors, peers, or suppliers
- `SELECT_cost` - Cost-based decision making
- `SELECT_compatibility` - Compatibility with existing systems or processes
- `SELECT_simplicity` - Preference for simple, easy-to-understand solutions
- `SELECT_proven` - Preference for proven, established technologies
- `SELECT_local` - Preference for locally available or supported solutions
- `SELECT_trial` - Pilot testing or trial periods before full adoption

#### 1.3 Implementation Process
**Definition:** The actual process of implementing and integrating innovations

**Codes:**
- `IMPL_phased` - Gradual, phased implementation approach
- `IMPL_complete` - Complete system overhaul or replacement
- `IMPL_parallel` - Running old and new systems in parallel
- `IMPL_training` - Training and skill development activities
- `IMPL_customization` - Adaptation or customization of solutions
- `IMPL_integration` - Integration with existing systems or processes
- `IMPL_testing` - Testing and optimization phases
- `IMPL_support` - External support or consulting during implementation

## CODING GUIDELINES

### General Principles
1. **Multiple Coding:** Segments can be coded with multiple codes
2. **Context Sensitivity:** Consider context when applying codes
3. **Granularity:** Use most specific code available
4. **Consistency:** Apply codes consistently across all data
5. **Evolution:** Allow codes to evolve as analysis progresses

### Coding Process
1. **First Pass:** Apply major themes and obvious sub-themes
2. **Second Pass:** Add detailed descriptive codes
3. **Third Pass:** Review for consistency and completeness
4. **Validation:** Cross-check coding with second analyst

### Code Definitions
- Each code should have clear definition and examples
- Maintain coding dictionary with examples from data
- Regular team discussions to ensure consistency
- Document coding decisions and rationale

### Quality Assurance
- Inter-coder reliability checks (minimum 20% of data)
- Regular coding team meetings
- Systematic review of coded segments
- Documentation of coding decisions and changes

## ANALYSIS TECHNIQUES

### Within-Case Analysis
- Develop individual case narratives
- Identify unique patterns and themes
- Map innovation journey for each case
- Analyze success factors and constraints

### Cross-Case Analysis
- Compare patterns across cases
- Identify common themes and variations
- Analyze by sector, size, and region
- Develop typologies and frameworks

### Thematic Analysis
- Identify recurring themes across data
- Analyze theme relationships and interactions
- Develop thematic maps and hierarchies
- Validate themes with participants

### Narrative Analysis
- Analyze innovation journey narratives
- Identify common story structures
- Examine turning points and critical events
- Understand causal sequences and relationships

## REPORTING FRAMEWORK

### Theme-Based Reporting
- Organize findings by major themes
- Use participant quotes to illustrate themes
- Provide quantitative summaries where appropriate
- Include negative cases and contradictions

### Case Study Approach
- Develop detailed case studies of exemplary cases
- Show innovation journey from start to finish
- Highlight key success factors and lessons learned
- Demonstrate practical applications of findings

### Comparative Analysis
- Compare findings across sectors and regions
- Identify patterns and variations
- Develop sector-specific recommendations
- Highlight contextual factors affecting outcomes

### Policy and Practice Implications
- Translate findings into actionable recommendations
- Address different stakeholder needs
- Provide evidence-based policy suggestions
- Offer practical guidance for SME owners and supporters
