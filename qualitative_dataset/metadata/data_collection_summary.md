## LIMITATIONS AND CONSIDERATIONS

### Sample Limitations
- **Geographic Coverage:** Limited to major urban centers
- **Sector Representation:** Some sectors underrepresented
- **Business Size:** Bias toward small and medium enterprises
- **Success Bias:** May overrepresent successful innovation adopters

### Data Collection Constraints
- **Time Constraints:** Limited follow-up opportunities
- **Language Barriers:** Some nuances may be lost in translation
- **Social Desirability:** Potential bias toward positive responses
- **Recall Bias:** Historical events may be imperfectly remembered

### Contextual Factors
- **Economic Environment:** Data collected during specific economic conditions
- **Policy Context:** Reflects current regulatory environment
- **Technology Landscape:** Rapid changes in technology availability
- **COVID-19 Impact:** Some responses influenced by pandemic experiences
