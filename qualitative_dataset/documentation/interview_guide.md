# Semi-Structured Interview Guide
## Innovation Adoption in Nigerian SMEs

### Interview Protocol
**Duration:** 45-60 minutes  
**Mode:** Face-to-face / Video call  
**Recording:** Audio with consent  

### Opening (5 minutes)
- Introduction and rapport building
- Explain research purpose
- Obtain consent for recording
- Assure confidentiality

### Section 1: Background Information (10 minutes)
1. Tell me about your business - what do you do and how long have you been operating?
2. What is your role in the company?
3. How many employees do you currently have?
4. What would you say is your main market - local, national, or international?

### Section 2: Innovation Journey (15-20 minutes)
5. Can you tell me the story of when you decided to adopt [specific technology/innovation]?
   - Probes: What triggered this decision? Who was involved?
   
6. Walk me through the implementation process.
   - Probes: Timeline? Phases? Critical moments?
   
7. What were the biggest hurdles you faced, and how did you navigate them?
   - Probes: Financial? Technical? Human resources? Regulatory?

### Section 3: Impact and Performance (10-15 minutes)
8. How has this innovation specifically affected your:
   - Sales/revenue?
   - Operational costs?
   - Customer relationships?
   - Market position?
   
9. Were there any unexpected outcomes - positive or negative?

10. How do you measure the success of this innovation in your business?

### Section 4: Enabling Factors and Constraints (10 minutes)
11. What external support did you receive, if any?
    - Probes: Government programs? Bank loans? Industry associations?
    
12. What role did your employees play in the adoption process?

13. If you were advising another SME owner about innovation adoption, what would you tell them?

### Section 5: Future Outlook (5 minutes)
14. What's next for your business in terms of innovation?

15. What changes in the business environment would make innovation easier for SMEs like yours?

### Closing
- Thank participant
- Explain next steps
- Provide contact information for follow-up
