## Research Study: Innovation Adoption and Constraints in Nigerian SMEs

### Study Information
**Research Title:** Leveraging Machine Learning to Examine Innovation Adoption and Constraints in Nigerian SMEs: Implications for Performance and Growth

**Principal Investigator:** [Research Team Name]
**Institution:** [University/Organization Name]
**Contact:** [Email and Phone]

### Purpose of the Study
You are being invited to participate in a research study examining how Nigerian Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) adopt innovations and the challenges they face. This study aims to understand the factors that enable or constrain innovation adoption and how this impacts business performance and growth.

### What Will Happen
If you agree to participate, you will be involved in:
- A semi-structured interview lasting approximately 45-60 minutes, OR
- A focus group discussion lasting approximately 90 minutes

The interview/discussion will cover:
- Your business background and operations
- Your experience with innovation adoption
- Challenges faced and strategies employed
- Impact on business performance
- Support systems and recommendations

### Voluntary Participation
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may:
- Decline to answer any question
- Take breaks as needed
- Withdraw from the study at any time without consequence
- Request your data be deleted up to 30 days after collection

### Confidentiality
- Your identity will be kept strictly confidential
- You will be assigned a participant code (e.g., INT001)
- Company names will be anonymized in publications
- Audio recordings will be transcribed and then deleted
- Data will be stored securely and accessed only by the research team
- Results will be reported in aggregate form

### Risks and Benefits
**Risks:** Minimal risk involved. You may experience minor discomfort discussing business challenges or failures.

**Benefits:** 
- Contribute to knowledge on SME innovation in Nigeria
- Receive a summary report of research findings
- Potential policy recommendations may benefit your sector
- Opportunity to reflect on your innovation journey

### Data Usage
- Data will be used for academic research and publications
- Anonymized excerpts may be used in reports and presentations
- Aggregated findings may inform policy recommendations
- Data will be retained for 5 years, then securely destroyed

### Audio Recording
With your permission, the session will be audio recorded to ensure accurate data capture. You may request the recorder be turned off at any time.

### Your Rights
- You have the right to review your transcript
- You may request corrections to factual errors
- You can withdraw consent up to 30 days post-interview
- You may contact the research ethics committee with concerns

### Questions or Concerns
If you have questions about:
- The research: Contact [PI Name] at [Email]
- Your rights: Contact [Ethics Committee] at [Email]
- Technical issues: Contact [Support] at [Email]
